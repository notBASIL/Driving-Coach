Data

How scoring works: each trip gets 1–5 stars on the same six factors TD lists (acceleration, braking, cornering, distractions, duration, speed). Events between 11 PM and 5 AM count 1.5× and trips over 90 minutes start losing duration points, matching TD's descriptions. TD's exact formula isn't public, so these are coaching estimates, not TD's numbers. Hard brake ≈ 0.30 g (strict) / 0.35 g (normal); fast start ≈ 0.25 / 0.30 g; sharp turn ≈ 0.30 / 0.37 g; speeding = 10+ km/h over the limit for 5+ seconds. Data stays on this phone only.
